## Pitch and visible work

The page frames communication barriers faced by Deaf and hard-of-hearing people in emergencies and when reading legal or medical documents. It describes a multi-module system for sign-language communication, emergency alerts, document interpretation, and an animated sign avatar. Its listed technologies include React, TypeScript, Gemini 3, and WebGL. These are **submission claims**; the research did not independently validate its translation accuracy or whether every described module worked.

## Judge-facing moment

**Inferred from the submission, not the unviewed demo:** a person signs or receives an urgent message and sees it translated into an accessible form. The problem has immediate stakes and a highly visual demonstration path.

## Transferable signal

Accessibility plus a visible transformation can be memorable. The unusually broad scope and precision claims are not a safe build template for a short solo sprint without a demonstrable evaluation.
