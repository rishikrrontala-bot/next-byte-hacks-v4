## Pitch and visible work

The page describes animal shelters coordinating foster placements through spreadsheets, group texts, and posts that get buried. FosterMatch's proposed solution is a single board where a shelter posts an animal, a foster filters by constraints, and a one-click claim locks the listing and reveals contact details for pickup. The authors explicitly describe it as a single-page HTML/CSS/JavaScript prototype whose data lives in the browser instead of a database.
