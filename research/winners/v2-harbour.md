## Pitch and visible work

The story starts with the authors' remote fishing village and the decision to leave shore when weather, crew fatigue, and financial pressure interact. Users place a pin on a map to view live marine conditions, risk factors, a go/caution/no-go recommendation, route options, and a 24-hour forecast slider. The described implementation is a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript app using MapLibre and Open-Meteo data.

## Judge-facing moment

**Inferred from the submission, not the unviewed demo:** move a map pin and see the safety recommendation change with the local conditions.

## Transferable signal

The first-place project used a simple stack to produce an immediate, legible decision. Its visual interface exists to expose a working calculation for a specific user.
