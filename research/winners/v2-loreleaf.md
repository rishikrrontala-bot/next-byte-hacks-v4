## Pitch and visible work

The story addresses students who benefit from studying alongside others but cannot always access a physical library. LoreLeaf describes a pixel-art online library where users take seats with avatars, run a Pomodoro timer, and contribute completed sessions to a communal growing tree. The listed stack is Next.js, Supabase, Tailwind, TypeScript, and Vercel.

## Judge-facing moment

**Inferred from the submission, not the unviewed demo:** completing a study cycle causes a shared tree to grow, making collective effort visible.

## Transferable signal

A playful, distinctive visual world can carry a real behavior loop. The visual treatment has a specific purpose: make otherwise invisible study companionship and progress feel present.
