## Sightline — selected

**Pitch:** Move a parked van and change approach speed to reveal how much road a driver has left after a person first becomes visible at a school crossing.

**Wow moment:** The 3D street shows the blocked sightline, then draws a stopping trace that extends past the crosswalk at one setting and stops before it after a change. The numbers derive from the same geometry and stopping model that drive the animation.

**Riskiest unknown:** Explaining a simplified sightline model honestly while making the 3D viewpoint intuitive on phones. The fallback is an equivalent top-down SVG diagram with the same calculated result. **First cut if late:** secondary narrative chapters, never the interactive comparison or demo video.

This fits V2/V3 winners' specific-user, visible-decision pattern. It does not repeat Rishik's earlier projects. The repo's kickoff brief delegates the concept pick; the current request asks for the product to be built if the repo is a sound starting point.

## Fine Print

**Pitch:** A playable subscription checkout exposes manipulative choices, then lets the user rewrite the flow clearly.

**Wow moment:** A one-click x-ray reveals hidden costs and cancellation friction in the same interface. **Riskiest unknown:** It could read like a polished static lesson rather than a technical tool. **First cut:** custom flow authoring.

## Quiet Map

**Pitch:** Students mark noise, crowding and lighting on a route to choose a more comfortable walk to class.

**Wow moment:** A route visibly changes as a student states what overwhelms them. **Riskiest unknown:** Without verified campus data, the demo would rely on invented map annotations. **First cut:** crowdsourced sharing.

The first two alternatives are concept pitches only. No claims of user testing or traction are made.
