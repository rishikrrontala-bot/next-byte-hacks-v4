# Proposed Sightline gallery — five product screenshots

These are capture instructions and paste-ready captions, **not** a claim that screenshots have been captured or uploaded. Use actual browser screenshots at a consistent desktop viewport. Avoid editor chrome, loading states, overlays that hide controls, or composited numbers. The scenario values below are displayed to one decimal place by the app.

1. **Hero — “A crossing can hide in plain sight.”**  
   Capture the full `#top` hero section (rather than a viewport-only crop) with the headline, **Run the crossing** action, image, and its figcaption.  
   **Caption:** “Sightline turns a hidden school-crossing sightline into an interactive lesson. The hero scene is a generated editorial illustration, not a photograph or measured street.”

2. **Default simulator — 31.5 m short.**  
   Capture `#experiment` after **Reset**: 25 mph, 12 m van setback. Frame the full street scene, both sliders, the result, and the 14.8 m view / 46.3 m stopping readout.  
   **Caption:** “At 25 mph with the van 12 m from the crossing, the model gives 14.8 m of continuous view and 46.3 m of reaction-plus-braking distance: 31.5 m short.”

3. **More room — 19.7 m remaining.**  
   At `#experiment`, click **More room**: 15 mph, 35 m van setback. Keep the changed van position, trace, both settings, and 43.1 m view / 23.4 m stopping readout in one frame.  
   **Caption:** “At 15 mph with 35 m of van setback, the model gives 43.1 m of continuous view and 23.4 m of stopping distance: 19.7 m remaining.”

4. **Three distances — inspectable pieces.**  
   With the 15 mph / 35 m case selected, capture `#method` so **First continuous view**, **Reaction travel**, and **Braking travel** are legible, including their current-case measurements. If the pinned layout requires it, capture after the third panel is in view.  
   **Caption:** “Sightline keeps first continuous view, reaction travel, and braking travel separate so visitors can question each part of the result.”

5. **Formula and evidence — assumptions in the open.**  
   Capture `#evidence` with **The model, openly**, the full stopping formula, the sentence naming fixed geometry and constant deceleration, and the FHWA/NHTSA source links.  
   **Caption:** “The calculation is disclosed: speed × 2.5 seconds for reaction, plus speed² ÷ (2 × 3.4 m/s²) for braking. FHWA design parameters support the lesson; this is no site survey or safety certificate.”

If the displayed values or layout change before capture, update the captions from the final app. Do not retain a number merely because it appears in this plan.
