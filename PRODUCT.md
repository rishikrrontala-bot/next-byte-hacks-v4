# Sightline product brief

- **Job and audience:** A student or community member lands on a public, no-login page and uses a street model to understand the relationship between an obstruction, first visibility, and available stopping distance.
- **Success:** In under a minute, they can change two controls, see the rendered scenario change, and describe why slowing down or moving the parked vehicle back changes the result.
- **Proof:** The output is calculated in-browser from disclosed geometry and FHWA design parameters. This is a transparent teaching model; no real street measurements or user traction are claimed.
- **Surface mode:** The first screen persuades and launches the product. The simulator is Operate: clear controls, legible measurements, keyboard access, and a reduced-motion fallback.
- **States:** WebGL available/unsupported, reduced motion, control bounds, result inside/beyond crossing, mobile layout. No authentication, network data, AI generation or user data storage.
